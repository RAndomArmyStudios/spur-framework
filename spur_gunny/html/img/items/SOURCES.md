# Where the gun pictures come from

RedM (spur_gunny, 25 Nov 2026): new. Every picture here is cut from a photograph that is in the public domain or
under CC0, lifted off its backdrop with `spur_core/dev/art/cut_photo.py`, turned and shrunk to 256 px. None is taken
from a game. A model with no file here shows the picture of its kind (`class/`).
